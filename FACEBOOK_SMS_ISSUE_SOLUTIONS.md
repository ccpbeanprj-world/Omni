> Facebook Business SMS verification only. This does **not** mean Messenger is connected to Omni. Live inbox: LINE + WhatsApp. [README.md](README.md)

**Problem**: Can't receive SMS for Facebook Business account verification  
**Impact**: Blocks Facebook app setup, not the current Omni LINE/WhatsApp product  
**Solutions**: Workarounds below

## Solution 1: Use Email Verification (Recommended)

### Try Email Instead of Phone

1. **During Signup**:
   - Look for "Verify with Email" option
   - Enter your business email
   - Check spam folder for verification email

2. **If Email Not Available**:
   - Use a different browser (Chrome/Firefox/Safari)
   - Clear browser cache/cookies
   - Try in incognito/private mode
   - Use VPN to different region (US/UK/Canada)

### Alternative: Use Existing WhatsApp (You Already Have!)

**Since Facebook Messenger is blocked, focus on what you have**:

✅ **WhatsApp via Twilio** - Already working!  
✅ **LINE** - Already working!

- Maximize WhatsApp/LINE integration
- Add WeChat later
- Skip Facebook for now

## Solution 2: WeChat Instead (Better for Your Region)

### Why WeChat Makes More Sense:

1. **No SMS Verification Issues**
   - Different verification process
   - Email-based for international accounts
   - More flexible options

2. **Better for Asian Markets**
   - 1.3 billion users
   - Strong in China, Asia-Pacific
   - Your current region likely better for WeChat

3. **Professional Features**
   - Official Account (服務號)
   - Payment integration
   - CRM features
   - WeChat Pay support

### WeChat Setup Process

**International Business (Without China License)**:

1. **WeChat Official Accounts Platform**:
   - Register at: https://mp.weixin.qq.com (Chinese)
   - Use email verification (no SMS for international)
   - Pay $300 USD annual fee
   - Submit business documents

2. **Required Documents**:
   - Business registration certificate
   - Business bank account statement
   - Company website
   - Business email domain

3. **Timeline**:
   - Registration: 1 week
   - Verification: 2-3 weeks
   - **Total: 3-4 weeks**

**Note**: This is actually FASTER than dealing with Facebook SMS issues!
